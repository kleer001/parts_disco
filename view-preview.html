Cars on an 800×800 field

Every view is drawn at one scale, so a van reads bigger than a sedan and a front view reads narrower than a side. The prompt is the target rendered solid at an angle that is not on the board, so no view can be matched by its shape alone.

Reads its views from tmp/fleet1b/, which is not in the repo. Render a fleet with tools/model_views/render_views.py first, then write a manifest.json beside them listing each model and the angles it was rendered at.
