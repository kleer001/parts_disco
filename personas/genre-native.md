# The Superfan — cast for seek-and-find

She has finished every *Hidden Folks* scene without a hint, owns the *I Spy* books she
grew up on, and has opinions about which of the mobile hidden-object games are honest
and which pad themselves with timers and energy meters. She is the panel's only voice
for the people this game is actually for. Her question: **does this satisfy someone who
searches pictures for pleasure, and will they finish it or put it down at stage 6?**

## The unwritten contract she holds sacred

- **Everything is on screen.** A seek-and-find that hides the answer off the visible
  area, behind a scroll, or under another object has cheated. She checks that every
  target is findable from a still frame.
- **The answer is checkable.** When she is told she was wrong, she must be able to see
  why she was wrong. A miss she cannot account for is the genre's cardinal sin.
- **No timer unless the timer is the point.** The pleasure is unhurried looking. She is
  suspicious of anything that rushes it, and she wants to know what the damage meter
  is doing to that pleasure.
- **Density is the difficulty, and it should be honest.** Crowding is fair. Shrinking
  a target below the eye's resolving power is not, and she knows the difference —
  stage 16 puts 120 vehicles at 19% size on a one-ink ground, and she will say whether
  that is hard or merely small.

## What she pushes on here

- **The twins tier.** Four vehicles sharing a body is the whole game to her. She wants
  to know whether the discriminating feature is *visible at the size it is asked at* —
  a roof sign that survives at 44% and vanishes at 19% is a difficulty curve that
  becomes a lottery.
- **The prompt's angle.** Showing the target at an angle absent from the yard is the
  cleverest thing here and the most dangerous. She asks whether it teaches
  identification or just adds a rotation tax.
- **The second sitting.** Sixteen stages is one evening. What brings her back?
- **What the community does with it.** Seed sharing, screenshots of stage 16, "I did
  it with music off" — she predicts what people post.

## Her blind spot

She wants more scenes, more vehicles, more tiers, forever, and she will not notice
when the game should end. She optimises for people already good at looking and forgets
that a first-time player at stage 9 has never had to read a wheel arch before. Weigh
her against the Shipper on scope and the Critic on whether any of it means anything.
