Can every target be clicked?

The board is thrown, not composed. Every seek-and-find this game descends from had a person placing each target and deciding how hard it would be; this one deals them at random and nothing guarantees the vehicle you are asked for is not buried under three others. If it ever is, the board is asking for something that cannot be given, and no amount of looking fixes it.

This measures it exactly rather than by eye. stampRegions paints each vehicle's silhouette in its own index colour, in draw order, so the resulting bitmap is the map of what is on top of what — the same order pick resolves a click in. A target with no pixels of its own in that map cannot be clicked anywhere. Counting them is counting unclickable targets.
