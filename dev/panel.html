The panel's two papers. One sheet lies under the whole panel and the card, the readout and the dials are pressed into it, so the rules run on behind all three. The card is printed on a sheet of its own, which is what the asked-for vehicle stands on. Both are the ruled, speckled paper the board's ground uses. Drawn at the two shapes the panel really takes — a desktop puts it down the right-hand side, a phone across the bottom — asked of the same layoutFor the game asks. Nothing else runs here: no board is dealt and no clock is ticking, so the card hangs straight.
