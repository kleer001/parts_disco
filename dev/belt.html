What does a moving board cost?

The still board is cached and blitted, which is why it costs 0.22ms a frame at any density. A conveyor belt takes that away — or looks like it does. This measures three ways of drawing a moving board so the choice is made on numbers rather than on the one figure that killed the idea last time.

Redrawn strokes every visible vehicle every frame, ground and all — the obvious way, and the one the 14fps figure came from. Strip renders the board once into a long canvas and blits it at an offset, which is what a scrolling game has always done. Strip, snapped is the same with the offset rounded to whole pixels: a one-pixel unantialiased line cannot move a third of a pixel, so an unsnapped blit resamples the linework every frame and a snapped one copies it exactly.
