What is actually in the packs

How this was made. Every model in every pack was traced by the game's own tool at four angles, and the silhouettes compared with each other: inspect_pack.py beside this page does the sorting, build_inspection.py draws it. The tiles are not artwork from the packs — they are the silhouette the board would hit-test against with its line art over the top, seen from the side. The photographs at the head of each pack are the publishers' own, served from their sites.

A model is thrown out for one of four reasons, in this order: it traced empty; it is not a member of the set (a wheel in a car kit); it fills too little of its frame or lies too long and thin to be worth clicking; or it is the same shape as something already kept. Everything else is kept, and the survey of where these packs came from is next door.
