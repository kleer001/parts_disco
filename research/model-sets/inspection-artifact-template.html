parts disco · model intake

Seven packs, sorted

How this was made. Every model in every pack was traced by the game's own tool at four bearings, its silhouette rasterised, and every pair compared by intersection over union. Two models above 0.95 are one shape — the board would be asking a question with two right answers — so one of the pair goes. Everything else stays, grouped with whatever it resembles. Nothing is thrown out for being small or thin.

The tiles are not artwork from the packs. Each is the silhouette the board would hit-test against with its line art over the top, seen from the side, drawn straight from the traced data. The photograph at the head of each pack is the publisher's own. Every pack here is CC0: Kenney's Car Kit, Cube Pets, Watercraft Kit and Food Kit, Quaternius's Cute Fish and Cars packs, and iPoly3D's Glasses Pack.
