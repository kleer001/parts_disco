the desk

The loop and the board's own sounds through one mixer, which is the only way to hear whether a find still cuts through. Both run on src/audio.js — the same module the game uses, the same voices, the same bus. Nothing here is a mock-up of the mix; it is the mix.

The music sits on its own fader with a duck between it and the master. Every effect presses it down and lets it back up, by an amount the effect carries: a find and a wrong click take the whole duck, a click on bare ground takes a third of one. Set depth to zero to hear what the loop does to the blips without it.
