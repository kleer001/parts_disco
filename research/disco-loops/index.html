disco loop auditions

Every candidate here is CC0 — public domain, no attribution owed — and every licence was read off that sound's own page, not taken from a search facet. Playback is Web Audio with loop = true, which is sample-accurate: what you hear at the join is what the file does, not a gap the player put there.

The two hosts hold different things. Freesound has loops — cut to a bar, usually with a bpm in the name. OpenGameArt's funk catalogue is mostly songs: they open cold, fade out and trail silence. Each card carries what was measured. Where a track is a song with a good groove in it, drag the region to loop a bar or two out of the middle and judge it that way.

Two things to know before shipping one. A Freesound card plays that site's -lq preview, because the master needs an account — fine to judge by, but fetch the master before it goes in the game. And a few OpenGameArt entries were re-encoded to ogg here purely to keep this page quick; their originals are untouched on disk.

Audio is not in the repository — it is a couple of hundred megabytes of other people's music. harvest.py, download.py and freesound.py in this directory fetch it into tmp/loops/audio/, six seconds apart, skipping whatever is already there; build.py measures it and writes loops.json.
