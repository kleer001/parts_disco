the four

The earmarked loops, rated on two things that are not the same kind of question. How well it loops is measured — bar length, the step across the join, the silence at the ends. How well it fits the game is a judgement, made against measurements but not derived from them; the reasoning is written out on each card so you can disagree with it.

All four play trimmed to whole bars by default, because none of them was cut to one. All four tick at that trim, so the crossfade switch is on — turn it off to hear what you would be shipping without it.

The step at the file's wrap below is the end of the whole file meeting its start, which is a join none of these ever plays. Measured at the trim instead — the wrap the game does play — the step is sharper than 100% of the track's own movement in Funky and Piano, 77% in Disco and 63% in Techno-ish. Read the number as a property of the download, not of the loop.

A correction on the earlier page: it called all four of these fades out. That was wrong. It compared the last 100ms against the first, which misreads any loop that leaves air before a downbeat. Measured over a fifth of the track, none of them fades — the ratios are 0.89 to 0.98.

Play it over the game

The only test that answers the second question. Start a loop above, then load the board and play a stage under it. The game's own clicks and blips keep working, which is the point — the in the game's band figure on each card is a guess at what you are about to hear, and this is the thing itself.

Numbers come from research/disco-loops/shortlist.py. Audio lives in tmp/loops/audio/ and is not in the repository — the fetch scripts in this directory put it there.
